## What NAVAREA2UC does

NAVAREA2UC reads navigational-notice text, interprets the published
meaning and geometry, and generates a derived ECDIS UserChart XML
representation. It does not distribute NAVAREA notices, official
message archives, nautical charts, or source-provider databases.

The source notice remains subject to the terms of its original
publisher or provider. NAVAREA2UC output is a derived technical
representation and does not replace official navigational
information or the mariner's obligation to verify current warnings.
