OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Implement the approved S3 repair:

Distance Values

Goal:

Prevent distance values from being interpreted
as numbered section markers.

Examples:

    14.5NM
    2.5NM
    0.6 MILES
    0.7 MILES

must NOT create:

    partition_id = 14
    partition_id = 2
    partition_id = 0
    etc.

CONFIRMED RCA
====================================================

    NAVAREA IX 546/2025

        14.

        group(1) = "14"

        partition_id = "14"

Channel geometry becomes fragmented.

====================================================
APPROVED DESIGN
====================================================

Approach A

Distance-aware exclusion
during marker collection.

Activation criteria:

    decimal value
    +
    distance unit

Supported units:

    NM
    NM.
    MILE
    MILES
    MI
    MI.

    14.5NM
    2.5NM
    0.6 MILES
    0.7 MILES

must be excluded from
numbered marker collection.
