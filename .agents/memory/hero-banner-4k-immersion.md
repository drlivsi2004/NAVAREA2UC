The landing-page hero should feel like an explorable ECDIS scene: visitors should want to enlarge it and inspect the chart details rather than treating it as a flat decorative image.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed that the 4096×4096 master creates the desired “fall into the banner” effect.

**How to apply:** Preserve the high-resolution master and its chart density for visual review. Avoid edits that flatten, oversoften, or over-saturate the map; keep future overlays and branding subordinate to the immersive chart composition.
